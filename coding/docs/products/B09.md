# B09 — Company Brief

Company Brief organizes company-level research, human fact verification, customer-fit assessment, and a reviewed email draft. It stores supplied company information and uses a fixed email template. It does not perform AI research, scrape websites, harvest individual contacts, look up private email addresses, or send outreach.

## Workflow

1. Create a brief with company name/website, ideal-customer-profile phrases, and a truthful description of your offer. Use comma-separated ICP phrases such as `logistics, warehouse`.
2. Add a company source: title, public URL, and relevant published text. The application does not fetch or independently verify the URL. Source and company website URLs reject malformed/out-of-range ports, credentials, whitespace, and control characters.
3. Add a concrete company fact with category, selected source, and exact supporting quote. The quote must occur in the stored source text (whitespace differences are allowed). A paraphrased statement can be entered, but is initially unverified.
4. Read the original source and verify that the statement is supported by its quote. Only explicitly verified facts enter assessment, research exports, or email drafts.
5. Read the literal ICP-phrase overlap assessment. It reports matching and missing phrases from verified facts; it is not an inferred qualification score. Make your own fit decision (`good`, `maybe`, or `poor`), explain it, and approve the research.
6. Generate the email. A fixed template includes up to three verified facts, numbered citations, the supplied company name, and your offer. Review or replace the subject and full body, then explicitly approve the email after checking its claims.
7. Export reviewed company facts as CSV, or reviewed research plus the approved email as text. The export is a draft package and does not transmit an email.

Changing a source marks linked facts `stale`, retains the old source version, and invalidates research/email approval. Update each stale fact against the current source before verifying it again, even if the old wording still occurs. Changing a fact resets verification; changing the profile, ICP, offer, or fact verification invalidates downstream reviews. Changing the company name or website additionally clears verification on every fact, so evidence for a previous company cannot silently carry over. Adding a source/fact also invalidates existing reviews conservatively. Reviewing research again marks any previous email as needing a fresh draft. Editing an email clears its approval. Source/fact histories and prior review snapshots are retained.

## API and data

Uses the shared private `/api/products/B09/records` API. Authentication, owner isolation, and optimistic revision checks are provided by the shared product router. Every action body includes the current `revision` at the API boundary. Domain functions return copies and ignore reserved metadata.

Create fields: `title`, `companyName`, `website`, `icp`, `offer`.

| Action | Business fields |
| --- | --- |
| `update-profile` | `companyName`, `website`, `icp`, `offer` (full replacement) |
| `add-source` | `title`, `url`, `content` |
| `update-source` | `sourceId` and the same full source fields |
| `add-fact` | `sourceId`, `category`, `statement`, `quote` |
| `update-fact` | `factId` and the same full fact fields |
| `verify-fact` | `factId`, `confirmed: true` |
| `review-research` | `fit: good/maybe/poor`, `notes`, `confirmed: true` |
| `draft-email` | none beyond shared revision |
| `edit-email` | `subject`, `body` |
| `review-email` | `confirmed: true` |

Fact categories: `overview`, `industry`, `location`, `size`, `operations`, `initiative`, `product`. Fact statuses: `unverified`, `verified`, `stale`. Record data includes company profile, `sources[]`, `facts[]`, `researchVersion`, `assessment`, `research`, and nullable `email`. Each fact carries a readable title, supporting quote, source title/URL/ID/version, and verification time. Research stores fit, notes, reviewed version, fact IDs, and review history. Email stores subject/body, citations, template description, research version, review history, and `draft`, `approved`, or `needs-refresh` status.

`csv` requires current reviewed research and exports only verified company facts with source/version/quote and fit rationale. `txt` additionally requires a current approved email. The shared JSON backup includes unverified facts and drafts for backup, and is not a reviewed delivery artifact. Spreadsheet-formula-leading CSV cells are escaped. Limits: 100 sources, 300 facts per company; 50,000 characters per source; 2,000 per fact/offer/ICP; 4,000 per quote; 12,000 per edited email body.

## Verification and remaining integrations

`cd coding/backend && .venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_products_b01_b09.py -q` verifies quotation integrity, explicit human verification, current-version review requirements, grounded template content, source/fact/profile invalidation, copy-on-write behavior, strict URL validation, and export gates. Human verification is an attestation; the application cannot prove that a supplied quote accurately represents the live page or that a paraphrase is faithful. Evidence retrieval connectors, CRM synchronization, personalized recipient lookup, and email delivery are not implemented.
